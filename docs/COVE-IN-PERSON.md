# In-person Cove sales — quick card

**Full Staff manual:** [STAFF-COVE-IN-PERSON-MANUAL.md](./STAFF-COVE-IN-PERSON-MANUAL.md)  
**In Staff Help:** `/staff?view=help&article=cove-in-person-manual`

---

**Sell first. One lane per sale. Never charge twice.**

| Lane | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Out** | Square Stand (all in-person tenders) |
| **In** | Portal / site — parent pays; Staff pickup only |

| Paying with… | Do this |
|--------------|---------|
| Cash or card / wallet | **Stand** → Cash or Card |
| Cove Photos QR | **Stand** → Gift card → scan |
| Cove **6-digit or word passcode** | **Stand** → search Customer → **Card on File** |
| Unable to load cards / no Cove load yet | Cash/card, or Staff Charge Cove backup |
| Portal / site (already paid) | **Today’s store pickups** → Handed out |
| Zelle / PayPal / phone (no Stand) | Staff → **External** |
| Membership | Portal only |

Cove balance = Square gift card. Portal checkout experience is unchanged if they prefer to pay online.
