# 48 — Where form emails go

**Audience:** All staff  
**Updated:** August 11, 2026  
**Also in Staff Help:** Knowledge base → Communications → “Where form emails go”

Use this when a parent asks “who got my message?” or when you wonder why your inbox did or did not get an alert.

All website and portal form emails send from the shared SHMS PTO Gmail mailbox. **Reply-To** is usually the parent, so Reply in Gmail goes to them. Change destinations in **Staff → Site settings → Contact**.

Dead aliases (`info@`, `membership@`, `marketing@`, `programs@`, etc.) auto-route to **president@shmspto.org**.

---

## Member Portal → Help (“Ask the PTO”)

Goes to **all three**:

1. **president@shmspto.org**
2. **vp-membershipexperience@shmspto.org**
3. **vp-marketing@shmspto.org**

Same for free and paid members. The request is also logged for follow-up.

---

## Public website forms → staff

| Form / page | Goes to (default) |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Contact (`/contact`) | president@ (general; topic does not auto-route) |
| Programs contact (`/programs`) | vp-initiatives@ and president@ |
| Event idea (`/events`) | vp-community-events@ |
| Sponsorship (`/fundraising`) | vp-sponsorships@ and president@ |
| Business owner (public fundraising section) | vp-membershipexperience@ |
| Volunteer signup (must be logged in) | vp-community-events@ |
| Newsletter signup | vp-marketing@, else president@ |
| Survey submit | vp-marketing@, else president@ |
| Board page email links | That officer’s email (opens mail app) |

Most submissions are also logged so staff can follow up in Staff.

---

## Member portal (free & paid)

| Action | Communication |
|--------|----------------|
| Help / Ask the PTO | Email → president + membership + marketing |
| Business owner form | Email → vp-membershipexperience@ |
| Invite another guardian | Email → **invitee**; Reply-To = primary parent |
| Program refund / transfer request | **No staff email** — status updated + parent gets a portal message |
| Profile, students, store card settings | No outbound staff email |

---

## Paid checkout (membership, Cove shop, store card, programs, events, donations)

| Who | What |
|-----|------|
| **Parent** | Order confirmation email + message in Member Portal → Messages |
| **Staff sale alert** | **All of:** vp-membershipexperience@, president@, treasurer@, cove@, cove-staff@, vp-sales@, secretary@ |

Membership alerts include shirt / magnet / refreshments fulfillment notes when relevant.

---

## Staff → parents

| Action | Goes to |
|--------|---------|
| Staff Messages / Membership outreach / Newsletter | Selected parents’ email + portal Messages |
| Password reset | Parent gets a reset email at their login address |

---

## Free vs paid

Routing is the **same**. Paid parents also get purchase confirmations; staff get sale alerts on successful checkout.
