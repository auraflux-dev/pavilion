Visitor, member-portal, and staff UI copy (Stone Hill, Commons demo, private trials):

- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) as punctuation. Use periods, commas, colons, or a new sentence.
- Money/time ranges: use `to` (`$20 to $50`, `Mon to Fri`, `8:25 to 8:50`). School years: ASCII hyphen (`2026-27`).
- Multi-beat copy: real line breaks (`\n`, `<br />`, or stacked `<p>`). Render with `whitespace-pre-line` when using `\n`. No orphan 1–2 word hangs.
- `humanizePublicCopy` strips CMS em/en dashes and capitalizes the next letter; still write source clean.
