# Wix Media: Uploading Media

Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager
Fetched: 2026-07-16
SHMS focus: Uploading images for products / board photos

> Short digest for PTO how-tos. Always prefer the live Wix article if UI differs.

• Click Media on the left side of the editor.
• Click the Upload Media icon .
• Click Open .
• Click My Wix Account .
• Click Upload Selection .
• Click Connect .
• Click the Link icon on the left side.
• Click + Upload Media .
