# Wix Stores: Managing Ribbons

Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-ribbons
Fetched: 2026-07-16
SHMS focus: Ribbons used as Deal of the Week on /store

> Short digest for PTO how-tos. Always prefer the live Wix article if UI differs.

• Click to add the name.
• Click the Ribbon field.
• Click More Actions then select Edit ribbon .
• Click + New Ribbon .
• Click the Checkmark icon .
• Click Save .
• Click Manage next to Ribbon .
• Click Edit next to the ribbon text you want to update. Tip: In the Products column, you can see how many products have this ribbon.
• Click Done .
• Click Settings or the Settings icon .
• Click the Settings tab.
• Click the relevant element in your editor.
• Click the Layout tab.
• Click the Design tab.
• Click More Actions and select Edit ribbon .
• Click Delete next to the temporary ribbon.
