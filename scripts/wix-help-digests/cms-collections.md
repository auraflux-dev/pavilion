- Managing your CMS collection content
• Go to CMS in your site's dashboard. Alternatively, you can click CMS in your editor, then click Your Collections .
• Click the relevant collection.
• Click + New View at the top.
• Click Manage Fields .
• Click Sort at the top of your collection.
• Click + Add a Sort .
• Click the Sort by drop-down and choose the field you want to sort.
• Click Add Sort .
• Click Filter at the top right.
• Click + New Filter .
• Click the Select Field drop-down and choose the field you want to filter.
• Click the Choose a Condition drop-down and select the criteria for your filter. The options vary depending on the field type.
• Click Add Filter .
• Click Mirror on Site at the top of the collection.
