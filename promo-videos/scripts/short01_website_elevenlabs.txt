Hi Stingray families. This year the Stone Hill Middle School PTO has a brand new website, built for busy parents.
You will find it at SHMSPTO dot org. You do not need a paid membership just to look around. Every family is welcome.
Programs are where evening enrichment lives when school is in session. Browse what is offered for students, and we will go deeper in a Programs video soon.
Events is where family nights and school celebrations show up. Check what is coming, and we will cover the full calendar in a later video.
The Cove is our snack window and spirit wear online. Families shop and load here, and we will walk the digital card in a Portal video.
Volunteer is for parents who want to help when they can. Sign up for roles that fit your schedule, no pressure.
Fundraising shows how the PTO stays funded. You can follow the goals and give when you are ready.
Meet the Board to see the parent volunteers who run SHMS PTO. Real people, local families.
PTO Meetings is where agendas and minutes live, so everyone can stay in the loop.
And when you are ready to support the PTO, Membership is right there in the menu. Here is the quick tour. Deeper tier details come in a Membership video.
