Hi Stingray families. Stone Hill Middle School PTO is run entirely by parent volunteers, and we have five board seats open right now.
We need people ASAP. Here is what each role does, and how to raise your hand.
Secretary keeps the PTO organized. You record meeting minutes, own the calendar and communications schedule, and match parents to short micro-tasks instead of huge commitments. About three to five hours a month, plus board meetings.
Treasurer is our financial steward. You track the budget, handle payments and reimbursements, keep insurance and tax filings current, and present clear monthly numbers to the board. About four to six hours a month.
SEAC Representative is our bridge for special education families. You attend monthly district SEAC meetings, bring updates back to SHMS, and help keep our events inclusive. This seat is appointed through LCPS, and we still want you to email the President so our board knows you are interested.
Events Coordinator supports the VP of Events. You help run inclusive family events, staff appreciation, school-led support, and bite-sized volunteer tasks busy parents can actually do. About two to four hours a month.
Initiatives Coordinator supports enrichment programs and sponsorships. You help with program logistics and tournaments, and assist outreach to local businesses so programs stay funded. About two to four hours a month.
Why serve on the board this year. These board positions receive free PTO membership, and seventy-five percent off enrichment programs.
If you are able, please support Initiatives with a donation on our site. It is optional, not a condition of serving.
Ready for a board seat. Go to the Board page on SHMSPTO dot org, and email president at SHMSPTO dot org. Tell us which role you want. We need you ASAP.
If you cannot join the board right now, that is okay. We still need you. Visit the Volunteer page, fill out the form, and offer any contribution of time that fits your schedule.
School store window, event support, chaperones, fundraisers, or whatever hour you can give. Sign up at SHMSPTO dot org slash volunteer.
Thank you, Stingray families. Go Stingrays!
