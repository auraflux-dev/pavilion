Open your laptop browser and go to SHMSPTO dot org slash staff. Sign in with Google using your cove at shmspto.org account, not a personal Gmail. This training covers snack window candy and in person spirit wear.
After sign-in, look at the dark green Staff top nav. Open The Cove. If you do not see it, tap More, then The Cove.
You are now on The Cove workspace. The top section is Cove register. That is Lane A for snacks and candy on a prepaid Cove Digital Card. Spirit wear with sizes usually sells on the iPad Square Stand, not this register.
Ask what they want. For snacks on the digital card, ask for their Photos or Wallet QR, their word passcode, or the six digit Family Cove backup code.
Type or paste that code into the box labeled Scan QR, GAN, or six digit backup. Press return or tap Lookup.
Check the screen. You should see the student full names and the family digital card balance. If the name is wrong, stop and look up again.
Tap each snack or candy tile to add it. Use plus and minus to change quantity. Watch the cart total and the remaining balance.
When the order looks right and the balance covers it, tap Charge. Hand over the snacks. The family card is now updated.
If the balance is too low for snacks, do not tap Charge. Tell them to reload online, or switch that snack sale to Square Stand. Spirit wear still goes on Stand.
Now spirit wear. Wake the Square Stand iPad on the SHMS PTO location. Ring shirts, hoodies, hats, and other merch as a normal card present sale. Confirm size before you charge. Guests with no Cove card also pay here. Never also Charge those same spirit items on Staff.
At PTO events, paid member refreshment perks are different. Ask for the six digit code. Paid member codes end in nine. Confirm on Staff if unsure, then hand tickets. Do not charge for that free perk. Extra snacks or spirit still go on Stand.
You now know both jobs. Snacks and candy on Staff register with the digital card. Spirit wear and guests on Square Stand. Never charge a family twice. Open Staff Help anytime for In person sales and Square Stand.
