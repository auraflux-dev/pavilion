Same two devices for The Cove snack window and for in-person PTO events. Staff on a laptop, Square Stand on the iPad.
Before you open the window or an event table, sign into Staff as cove at shmspto.org, open The Cove, and wake Square Stand on the right location.
Lane A is a prepaid Cove Digital Card. Enter the six digit family code or Wallet QR number, confirm the kids and the balance, then Charge snacks or event catalog items on Staff.
If the balance is too low, stop. The parent reloads online, or you switch that sale to Square Stand.
Lane B is guests with no card, plus card present spirit wear and event merch. Ring those on Square Stand.
At events, paid member refreshment perks use a family code that ends in nine. Check the code, hand the tickets, and do not charge again for that perk. Never charge a family twice. Full steps are in Staff Help.
