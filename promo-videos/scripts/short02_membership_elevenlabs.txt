Lagoon is one hundred forty nine dollars, and it is our most popular. Bigger perks, more card credit, and event hospitality.
Tide is two hundred forty nine dollars, our top tier, with the strongest discounts and partner perks when available.
For a limited time, the first thirty days add a ten percent bonus on that card credit only.
