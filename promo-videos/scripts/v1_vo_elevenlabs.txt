Hi Stingray families. Sixth, seventh, and eighth grade.
This year we launched a brand-new Stone Hill Middle School PTO website:
shmspto.org.

One place for the whole school year.
Memberships that show real value.
A Member Portal for your family.
And a Cove Digital Card your kids can use at the snack window,
with a code and a QR right on your phone.

We have never offered memberships this clearly before.
We have never had a Cove Digital Card like this.
So we are going to walk the site slowly.
Free account versus paid membership.
Every Reef, Lagoon, and Tide benefit.
Then every feature inside the Member Portal.
Enrichment program discounts show up as a membership perk here.
The full enrichment tour is a separate video.

Start with free.
Create a free parent account. Log in or sign up on shmspto.org.
Free is not a tease. Free unlocks the Member Portal itself.
With free you can add your students, open The Cove, load a family Cove Digital Card with your own money,
join your grade WhatsApp, answer surveys, and read Member Help.
You see the same portal layout paid members see.

Paid means you choose Reef, Lagoon, or Tide for the school year.
Paid keeps every free tool, and adds prepaid card credit plus year long perks.
Your account banner changes from Free parent account to Paid PTO membership active,
and each student card can show your tier.

Here is Reef. Seventy nine dollars for the school year.
You get a twenty dollar prepaid PTO card.
And for a limited time in the first thirty days, that credit loads as twenty two dollars with the ten percent bonus.
You get ten percent off enrichment programs for the entire school year.
And you get one magnet from our online store.

Here is Lagoon. One hundred forty nine dollars. Our most popular.
You get a forty dollar prepaid PTO card. Forty four dollars loaded with the first thirty day bonus.
You get fifteen percent off enrichment programs all year.
Free food and refreshments at all PTO events.
One T shirt from spirit wear.
And one magnet.

Here is Tide. Two hundred forty nine dollars.
You get a seventy five dollar prepaid PTO card. Eighty two fifty with the first thirty day bonus.
You get thirty percent off enrichment programs all year.
Free food and refreshments at all PTO events.
One T shirt, one magnet,
and exclusive discount options from partnered local businesses.

Faculty and staff can join for fifteen dollars for the school year. Email us to join.
Memberships run August through June. Renew each fall.

Now open the Member Portal after you sign in.
This is home base for your family.

At the top you will see jump links.
Calendar and Messages.
Store and Cove Digital Card.
Surveys.
And Help.

My Account shows whether you are free or paid,
your email, edit profile, member since date,
how you pay at the snack window versus online reloads,
and the Grade WhatsApp buttons. Join sixth, seventh, or eighth,
for reminders and PTO updates. That is for every signed in parent, free or paid.

My Students is where you add every child in your household.
First name, last name, grade six, seven, or eight.
On each student card you will see store card balance for that family account,
and if you are paid, your membership tier badge.
You can edit a student, load funds, and turn auto top off on or off later if you want.

Calendar and Messages fill in as your students enroll in programs.
Session times, school events, and instructor messages in one inbox.
If it looks empty at first, that is normal until enrollments start.

Store and Cove Digital Card is the new digital card experience.
You will see your current family balance,
recent purchases,
and your Family Cove code. A six digit number, plus a QR code.
Students say the code at The Cove window, or show the QR, so staff can charge the family balance.
You can load the family card online anytime. Twenty, forty, seventy five, or any whole dollar amount,
with card or PayPal.
If you bought Reef, Lagoon, or Tide, your membership credit appears here first.
Reloads after that are dollar for dollar.
Optional plastic cards are for faster tapping at the window. Same balance either way.

Surveys for you stay on shmspto.org. The same branded form we send by email, text, or WhatsApp.
No random outside links.

Member Help is your knowledge base.
Account, students, membership, The Cove, and programs. Written for signed in parents.

From the portal you can also open Membership to upgrade,
The Cove to shop spirit wear and see the snack menu,
and Programs when you are ready to enroll. Again, the deep program tour is a separate video.
Paid members often see Cove coupons when signed in. Look for the coupon bar when shopping.

That is the whole path.
Free account to enter the portal and use the Cove Digital Card.
Paid Reef, Lagoon, or Tide if you want prepaid credit and the year long benefits we just listed.

Go to shmspto.org, create your free account, then choose the tier that fits your family.
Go Stingrays.
