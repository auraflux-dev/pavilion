Hey Diane. Sign in at SHMS PTO dot org slash staff with your Google account — vp-marketing at shmspto.org.
This is your lane. You never write HTML. Designs live in Canva. Words live in plain text boxes here in Staff Newsletter.
Once you're in, tap Newsletter up top. You'll land on Templates — Canva plus copy.
Paste a Canva link or grab a recent design, then hit Export PNG for email. If Canva has multiple pages, each one comes through. Save it as a template when you'll use that layout again.
Before anything goes to parents, scroll to Test send. Pick Just me or the board, preview who gets it, then send a test. Subject shows TEST in brackets, and nothing hits the parent portal.
Next, choose who this is for. Paid members get your full email — Canva image plus your plain text. Weekly Scoop is the free monthly link, not the full design. Footer signups stay email-only.
Running a Weekly Scoop? Paste the Canva view link, then use Copy plus open WhatsApp and Post scoop to portal. You'll attach the PNG yourself in WhatsApp — Meta won't let us auto-post.
Want structure without HTML? Check Write in beats and fill intro, Event, Ask, CTA, and sign-off. Still plain text. firstName and tier merge tags work in subject or body if you want them personal.
Write your subject and body, set the UTM campaign, and leave click tracking on. Turn on open tracking when you've got a Canva hero PNG.
When you're ready for a paid send: Preview recipients, then Send email now. Bigger lists will ask you to type SEND so nothing goes out by accident.
Need it later? Schedule a Send at time in your local clock. Marketing queues wait for secretary or president approval, then the job goes out within about fifteen minutes of that time.
After it sends, open Send report on the same page and hit Refresh — delivered, failed, opens, and which links got clicked.
Keep it simple: no HTML, always test first, Weekly Scoop for the free monthly link, and attach the PNG by hand in WhatsApp. The full walkthrough with screenshots lives in Staff Help under Member newsletter for Diane. You've got this.
