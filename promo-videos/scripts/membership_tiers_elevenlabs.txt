Hello Stingray families. We have been working on some amazing memberships for your families. If you've never looked at Reef, Lagoon, or Tide before, this video walks through what each perk means and roughly what it's worth — so you can decide what fits.
Buying is the path. On SHMSPTO dot org, tap Join on Reef, Lagoon, or Tide. If you're new, create your parent account with your personal email or Google, then pay for your tier in the same flow — that purchase makes you a paid member for the school year. You do not have to start as free first. A free account is optional — only if you want the Member Portal without buying a paid membership.
Three value ideas before the tiers. The Cove Digital Card is your family's PTO wallet — use it at The Cove in person, or online for enrichment, events, donations, spirit wear, and other PTO checkout. You can load your own money anytime. When you buy a paid membership, we add starter credit on top of whatever's already there.
Enrichment means seasonal PTO programs outside the school day — fall and spring. Pricing is still being set, but they average about three hundred fifty dollars a season, so member discounts are real money. Free refreshments on Lagoon and Tide means you don't pay for PTO event snacks or food-truck food — about thirty dollars saved for a family of three or four at each event.
Spirit wear: the included T-shirt is about eighteen dollars in the shop. The Stone Hill car magnet is ten dollars in the spirit shop — and Lagoon and Tide memberships include both the shirt and the magnet.
Reef is seventy nine dollars. You get twenty dollars on the Cove card — twenty two with the limited first thirty day ten percent bonus — ten percent off enrichment, about thirty five dollars per typical season, and one magnet.
Lagoon is one hundred forty nine — most popular. Forty dollars on the card — forty four with the bonus — fifteen percent off enrichment, about fifty two dollars per season, free event refreshments, one T-shirt, and one magnet.
Tide is two hundred forty nine. Seventy five on the card — eighty two fifty with the bonus — thirty percent off enrichment, about one hundred five dollars per season, same free refreshments, shirt, and magnet, plus local partner discounts still being lined up.
Teachers and staff: Faculty membership is twenty dollars for the school year. You choose one included perk — the Stone Hill car magnet or a Spirit Wear T-shirt — then pay on the page.
Here's the purchase for parents. Lagoon and Tide ask for a shirt size, then you pay on the page — and you still get the magnet too. Memberships run August through June. The card bonus is credit only for the first thirty days. And joining does not mean mandatory volunteer hours.
After you pay, this is the Member Portal as a paid member. You will see Paid on My Account. Your tier badge sits on your student. Membership credit lands on the Cove Digital Card. Family Cove codes end in nine.
Compare the tiers on SHMSPTO dot org. Your membership funds what the PTO runs — and gives value back the same year. Next up: enrichment programs. Go Stingrays!
