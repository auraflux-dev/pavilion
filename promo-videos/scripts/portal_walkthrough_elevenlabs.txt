Hey Stingray families. If you're a parent who hasn't used the Member Portal yet, this video is for you. In the next couple of minutes I'll show you why it matters, how to get set up free, what paid memberships add, and how to find us at Open House — so you leave knowing exactly what to do.
Here's the everyday reason to care: your kids buy snacks at The Cove in person. Cash still works — but the Cove Digital Card is the better option. Load from home, skip digging for bills at the window, and keep one family balance ready. The Member Portal is how you set that up before they get in line.
Start free. Join or log in with your personal email or Google. That unlocks the portal — you don't have to buy a membership first.
Once you're in, this is your Member Portal home. At the top is your family setup checklist — that's your first-time path. It shows what's done and what's left before the Cove Digital Card unlocks.
My Account shows whether you're free or paid. You can edit your profile here, and this is also where WhatsApp reminders live if you want them.
Checklist step one: add your kids under My Students. Then finish each safety profile — your phone, an emergency contact, and who's allowed to pick them up. When safety is done, the checklist unlocks the Cove Digital Card. Until then, the card stays locked.
The Cove Digital Card is the smoother way to pay at the snack window. Cash is fine if that's what you have — but with the card you load money online, then they show the QR or say the backup code at The Cove. Reload anytime.
When you're ready for more, paid memberships are Reef, Lagoon, or Tide. You get dollars on the Cove Digital Card to start, spirit wear based on your tier, and free refreshments for the family at PTO events — plus other year-long perks. We won't unpack every tier here — that's another video.
Here's the same portal as a paid member. You'll see Paid on My Account, a tier badge on your student, membership credit already on the Cove card, and paid Family Cove codes that end in nine.
Paid also unlocks enrichment discounts and priority registration. Staff recognize paid families by that code ending in nine.
Some tools work the same either way. Calendar and Messages fill in after enrichment enrollments — empty at first is normal. Member Help has short how-tos, and you can Ask the PTO if you're stuck.
Spirit wear is under Stingrays Pride — order online, pick up at school. Same shop whether you're free or paid.
Come see us Thursday at Open House in the cafeteria. The PTO provides one free refreshment ticket for the food truck. We can talk through everything PTO while you look at spirit wear.
Try the free portal today on SHMSPTO dot org, then come find us in the cafeteria. Go Stingrays!
