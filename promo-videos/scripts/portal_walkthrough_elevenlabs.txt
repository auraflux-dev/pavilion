Hi Stingray families.
This week is Open House. Thursday August thirteenth.
Find Stone Hill Middle School PTO in the cafeteria.
Sixth grade nine to eleven. Seventh and eighth one to three.
Spirit wear. Memberships. Member Portal. Cove Digital Card. Enrichment info.
And Sips and Sweets food truck tickets. one per family at our table.
Paid members get free refreshment tickets for the whole family.

This short video walks the Member Portal so you know what we will show you in person.
and so you can try it tonight on shmspto.org.

Start free.
Create a free parent account. Join or Log in. personal email or Google.
Free unlocks the Member Portal itself. same layout paid members see.
Add your students. open The Cove. load a Cove Digital Card. join grade WhatsApp. answer surveys. read Member Help.

Paid is Reef, Lagoon, or Tide for the school year.
Same portal tools. plus prepaid card credit and year long perks.
Your banner changes from Free parent account to Paid PTO membership active.

After you sign in, open Member Portal. this is home base.

Jump links at the top. Calendar and Messages. Store and Cove Digital Card. Surveys. Help.

My Account. free or paid status. profile. how you pay at the snack window. Grade WhatsApp for sixth, seventh, or eighth.

My Students. add every child. first name, last name, grade.
Each card shows family store balance. Paid shows your tier badge.

Calendar and Messages fill in after program enrollments. empty at first is normal.

Store and Cove Digital Card.
Family balance. recent purchases. your six digit Family Cove code and Q R.
Kids say the code or show the Q R at The Cove window.
Load online anytime. Membership credit appears here first if you are paid.
Paid Family Cove codes end in nine. that is also how we know you for free food truck tickets at Open House.

Surveys stay on shmspto.org. Member Help is your knowledge base.

Come see us Thursday in the cafeteria, or go to shmspto.org now and create your free account.
Go Stingrays.
