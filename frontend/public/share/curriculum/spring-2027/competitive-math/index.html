Competitive Math Prep

Janet Bih · Thursday 6:00 to 7:00 PM

Spring curriculum — Advanced systems & scrimmages

Twelve weekly sessions. Curriculum only.

  1. Week 1: Advanced number theory

    Base conversions, multi-concept remainder systems, digit logic.

  2. Week 2: Advanced algebra & systems

    Systems of equations, strategic substitutions, quadratic applications.

  3. Week 3: Competition geometry II: area

    Circle geometry, similar triangles, coordinate geometry, shadow methods.

  4. Week 4: 3D geometry & spatial reasoning

    Surface area, nets, volume of composite solids, spatial visualization.

  5. Week 5: Advanced counting & casework

    Restricted arrangements, complementary counting, pigeonhole principle.

  6. Week 6: Unlabeled problem solving

    Identifying hidden math domains in ambiguous competition problems.

  7. Week 7: Sprint round simulation

    High-pressure, 40-minute, 30-problem no-calculator timed speed run.

  8. Week 8: Target round deep-dive

    Eight complex multi-step problems with full student solution presentations.

  9. Week 9: Team round dynamics & tactics

    Workload division, cross-checking protocols, collaborative practice.

  10. Week 10: Head-to-head countdown scrimmage

    Rapid-fire, buzzer-style oral speed round under time pressure.

  11. Week 11: Full mock competition

    Integrated Sprint, Target, and Team round simulation.

  12. Week 12: Final showcase & awards

    Post-assessment growth review, parent presentation, awards.
