Fall curriculum — Foundations & speed mechanics

Twelve weekly sessions. Curriculum only.

  1. Week 1: Diagnostic & speed mindset

    Baseline assessment, mental estimation, working backward, answer checking.

  2. Week 2: Number theory: factors & divisibility

    Prime factorization, GCF/LCM shortcuts, divisor counting, digit sums.

  3. Week 3: Number theory: modular arithmetic

    Remainders, units digits, clock arithmetic, repeating cyclical patterns.

  4. Week 4: Sprint speed hacks (no calculator)

    Rapid mental math, fraction-decimal conversions, squaring tricks.

  5. Week 5: Algebraic expressions & manipulations

    Linear equations, combining like terms, algebraic identities, shortcut evaluation.

  6. Week 6: Word problem decomposition

    Rate-time-distance, work problems, proportions, mixtures, age problems.

  7. Week 7: Sequences & series

    Arithmetic and geometric sequences, term formulas, series summation tricks.

  8. Week 8: Combinatorics & counting

    Permutations, combinations, Venn diagrams, fundamental counting principle.

  9. Week 9: Probability & expected value

    Dependent and independent events, geometric probability, probability trees.

  10. Week 10: Competition geometry I: angles

    Angle chasing, triangle properties, parallel lines, Pythagorean shortcuts.

  11. Week 11: Target round strategy (calculator)

    Multi-step problem decomposition, calculator judgment, time budgeting.

  12. Week 12: Mid-year Sprint/Target showcase

    Timed individual assessment, parent-facing scorecard, error analysis.
