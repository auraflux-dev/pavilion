Fall curriculum (Loudoun Robotics proposal)

Twelve weekly sessions. Curriculum only.

  1. Week 1: Hardware assembly

    Kit orientation and driving base

  2. Week 2: Structural integrity

    Bracing and load paths

  3. Week 3: Gear ratios

    Speed vs pulling power

  4. Week 4: Programming motors

    Sequence and exact distance

  5. Week 5: Programming loops

    Repeatable paths and turns

  6. Week 6: Force / touch sensors

    Stop on contact

  7. Week 7: Color / light sensors

    Line detection

  8. Week 8: Distance sensors

    Stop before an obstacle

  9. Week 9: Logic pathways

    If/else with two sensors

  10. Week 10: Driver vs autonomous

    Remote then pre-programmed

  11. Week 11: Team design challenge

    Simple attachment on a mat task

  12. Week 12: Troubleshooting & Fall showcase

    Final runs for families
